points

Give every action a value

point types

Your own currencies — Gems, Coins, whatever fits

Create unlimited point types with singular and plural names and an icon from 30 presets — or upload your own. Each type keeps its own totals, its own leaderboard filter, and can even drive its own rank ladder.

point triggers

Decide exactly what earns and what costs

Every point type carries its own trigger list: “Get reactions on activity posts (x20)”, “Get accepted to private groups (x2)”, “Join specific group type”. Each row shows its audience, its value, and a toggle to pause it — and triggers can move between types whenever you reorganize.

leaderboard

A leaderboard members check on purpose

The Awards page ranks the community by any point type — Total points, Gem, Engagement, Learning — across all time or the last 7, 30, or 90 days. A pinned “(You)” row means every member always knows where they stand, and the top three wear medals.

achievements

Every badge has a story behind it

achievements

Meta-badges for your most dedicated members

Drag to reorder, toggle any badge on or off, and read each criteria line at a glance — “Join your first groups (x5)”, “Earn 2000 total points”, “Earn Level 5 rank”. Disabling achievements hides earned badges from the community, but nothing is deleted.

achievement triggers

Four ways to unlock: actions, points, ranks — or other achievements

Choose the trigger style per badge: perform an action N times, cross a point threshold in the type you pick, reach a rank level, or complete other achievements. Icons come from the same 30-preset gallery — and triggers lock after saving, so earned badges stay fair.

achievement icons

Badges members actually show off

Thirty preset icons — medals, crowns, gems, rockets — or your own upload. Earned badges follow members everywhere: profile headers, directory cards, and hover cards.

recognition

Recognition everywhere members look

profile header

Points, badges and rank ride on every profile

Beneath the name sits one chip per currency — “30 Gems”, “1100 Engagements” — then a row of earned achievement medallions, all above Connect, Follow and Message. A numbered rosette pins to the avatar.

display settings

Points and badges wherever members appear

Profile headers and member-directory cards each get their own Points, Ranks, and Achievements toggles — six switches deciding how much status shows in public, and where.

award page

One page for the whole reward economy

Create or assign the community Awards page and members get Leaderboard, Ranks, Achievements, and Competitions tabs in one destination — styled by ReadyLaunch like the rest of your site.

manual award

Hand out (or take back) rewards by hand

Search any member by @username, see their balances per point type with the total, then award or deduct points and grant achievements on the spot. A recent-activity log keeps every manual change on the record.
