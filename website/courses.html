discover

Find the course before you commit to it

COURSE DIRECTORY

Every course your LMS publishes, listed in one place

The directory renders the catalogue as cards — cover image, two-line title, category, and an instructor byline that reads "By" plus the name. A count label sits at the top left, so the size of the catalogue is clear before scrolling.

HOVER PREVIEW

Preview a course without leaving the directory

Hovering a card floats a panel with the update date, a meta line of lessons, hours and level, a short excerpt and the instructors. Not-enrolled courses end with your LMS’s "Add to Cart" and "Buy Now"; enrolled ones show a single "Continue".

WISHLIST

Save a course from the cover image

Disallow editing after a set number of minutes (default 5), so replies can’t be rewritten after the conversation has moved on. Typos get fixed; history stays intact.

ENROLLMENT BADGE

A status pill shows enrolment before opening a course

A tinted pill sits at the top left of the cover reading "Not Enrolled". Cards in this style trade the price row for a multi-category line and a three-line excerpt, so enrolment state is legible straight from the grid.

choose

Approval, scheduling, and where a post lands

course header

The course page opens with what identifies the course

The hero leads with the category line, the title, a star rating with its count in parentheses, the language, and the instructor byline. One block answers what the course is and who teaches it.

preview video

A preview video anchors the hero card

Symbolic links create secure “shortcuts” to member uploads, optimizing performance; if you turn them off, a fallback method protects your files. A green “Symbolic links are enabled” notice confirms the healthy state.

instructors

Every instructor on the course, with their record

Below the summary, an "Instructor" heading lists each teacher as an avatar, a name, and a meta line carrying a star rating, its review count and a course count. A course can list more than one.

ABOUT COURSE

The full description sits below the curriculum

"About course" renders the description from your LMS in full — what is covered, who it suits, where it leads. Nothing is clamped and there is no read-more, so the whole description shows on the page.

learn

Once they are in, the page changes.

course content

The curriculum expands section by section

"Course content" lists tinted section headers with their lesson counts and a "Collapse All Sections" control. Each row shows the content-type icon its LMS item carries — video, quiz, assignment, document — a duration, a padlock when locked, and a completion circle once enrolled.

STICKY COURSE BAR

A condensed bar keeps the action in reach

Scrolling past the hero swaps in a slim bar under the nav: video thumb, title, rating, language and byline on the left; price and "Buy Now", or the completion percentage with a mini bar and "Continue", on the right.

Progress bar

The hero becomes a progress meter after enrolling

Choose which actions appear across member cards and profile headers, and which one leads as the primary button — the others tuck in as secondary. One decision, consistent everywhere members meet.

lesson player

A player built for the lesson, not a plugin page

Your LMS’s lesson opens in the community’s own player — scrubber, speed control, picture-in-picture and fullscreen — under a heading that counts the lesson’s place in the course. A primary "Complete Lesson" button and "Previous" / "Next Lesson" controls close the page.

assingment

Assignments arrive with their brief and their files

An assignment from your LMS renders its title and a meta row carrying duration, deadline, total marks and passing grade. The brief sits under "Description", downloadable files under "Attachments", and the learner starts or skips from two buttons at the foot.

quizzes

A quiz states its terms before the first question

The start card names the quiz, then lists what your LMS set — how many questions, attempts already made, total marks, passing grade, and whether there is a time limit. "Start Quiz" and "Skip Quiz" sit beneath, so nobody begins without knowing the rules.

connect

Learning sits beside everything else

my courses

Courses share a rail with groups and the feed

The community sidebar runs "News Feed", "Members" and "Groups", then "My Groups", then "My Courses" — enrolled courses as thumbnail rows with truncated titles and the active one filled. Learning is a nav item, not a separate site.

recently enrolled

See who else is in the course is enrolled already

A tinted pill sits at the top left of the cover reading "Not Enrolled". Cards in this style trade the price row for a multi-category line and a three-line excerpt, so enrolment state is legible straight from the grid.

reviews

Member reviews close out the course page

A tinted "Review" block pairs the aggregate score and its star row with a two-by-two grid of testimonials — avatar, member name, stars and date on each — followed by a "See all Reviews" button.
