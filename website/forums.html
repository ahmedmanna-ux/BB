discuss

Start a board. Start a conversation.

forum directory

A forums directory that leads with what matters

Choose what the Forums directory shows: the Forum Index, or Discussions by Last Post. The frontend renders a card grid — covers, descriptions, tags, and activity meta on every forum.

reply threading

Threaded replies that keep long arguments readable

Enable threaded (nested) replies and choose how many levels deep they nest — two by default. In ReadyLaunch every reply quotes the message it answers, so long arguments stay readable at any depth.

forum features

Every group, dressed for your community

favorites

Save your favorite discussions

Allow members to mark discussions as favorites with one click in the thread header — and the forum header carries a Favorite button of its own, so whole boards can be bookmarked too.

discussion tags

Tags that cross-cut every board

Allow members to mark discussions as favorites with one click in the thread header — and the forum header carries a Favorite button of its own, so whole boards can be bookmarked too.

revision log

Revision logs for every discussion and reply

Allow discussion and reply revision logging, so every edit leaves a trail. When members edit, the thread shows “Last edited 10 minutes ago” right beside the timestamp.

search

Forum-wide search across every board

Allow forum-wide search, and every forum, discussion, and reply becomes findable. It plugs into the community’s global search — one query, the whole archive.

post formatting

A formatting toolbar for real writing

Add a toolbar and buttons to text areas to help with HTML formatting — bold, italics, lists, quotes, links, and code. The frontend composer adds photos, video, attachments, GIFs, emoji, and @mentions.

groups & urls

Where boards live, and what they’re called

group forums

A discussion board inside any social group

Allow social groups to have their own forums — a structured Discussions tab beside the group feed, sub-forums included. Group context stays: the About panel, the members widget, and a New Discussion button.

forum permalinks

Six URL slugs, every one yours

Custom URL slugs for forum content — forum, discussion, topic-tag, view, reply, and search. Lowercase letters, numbers, and hyphens; your information architecture, not ours.

manage

Who creates, who joins, who runs it

disallow editing after

An editing window that keeps threads honest

Disallow editing after a set number of minutes (default 5), so replies can’t be rewritten after the conversation has moved on. Typos get fixed; history stays intact.

THROTTLE POSTING EVERY

Posting throttled to stop the flooders

Throttle posting every few seconds (default 10) so no member — or bot — can flood a discussion. Regular typing never notices; rapid-fire spam hits a wall.

DISCUSSIONS AND REPLIES PER PAGE

Page sizes tuned for your board’s pace

Choose how many forums (15), discussions (15), and replies (25) load per page. Long boards stay fast, and the frontend paginates with Prev · 1 2 3 … 15 · Next.

PER RSS PAGE

RSS feeds sized separately from your pages

Set how many discussions (15) and replies (25) appear per RSS page — feed readers get their own pagination, independent of the site. Subscribers stay current without hammering your server.

manage forums

Admin lists that run every board from wp-admin

Forums, Discussions, Discussion Tags, and Replies — four managed lists with search, filters, sort, and bulk edit. Row quick-actions reach Spam, Close, and Trash without leaving the queue.
