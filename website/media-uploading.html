photos & videos

Every picture in its place

photos

Photo uploads on every surface you allow

Four toggles decide where photos live: profiles and activity posts, groups, private messages, and forum discussions and replies. In the feed, multi-photo posts lay out in smart galleries — two-up grids or one-large-plus-thumbnails.

albums

Albums members curate themselves

Nested “Enable albums” toggles switch curation on for profiles and groups. Album cards carry privacy, a created date, and separate photo and video counts — and members can create an album mid-upload without leaving the flow.

upload size

Set the ceiling, per media type

Photos, videos, and documents each get their own cap in megabytes — “Set max file size for photo uploads… Your server’s maximum upload size is 256 MB.” Big enough for real work, small enough to protect your server.

upload limit

Batches, not floods

Cap how many items can join one activity post or upload — ten per batch by default. The composer shows every queued photo with one-tap remove, so members curate before they post.

videos

Native video in feeds, groups, messages, and forums

The same four-context control as photos. Tiles get play overlays and duration badges, uploads land straight in the composer, and playback opens in the full theatre view.

video file extensions

Choose the formats you trust

Toggle .mp4, .webm, .ogg, .mov, .m4v, and .avi individually — “For best performance and compatibility, we recommend MP4 and WebM.” Add your own extensions, and remove the ones you added.

DOCUMENT FILE EXTENSIONS

An allowlist you control, down to the MIME type

Tick extensions on or off — .abw AbiWord to .htm HTML — or add your own with a description, an icon, and a MIME type. Not sure of the MIME? Upload a sample file and click “Get MIME Type.”

emoji & gifs

Say it with more than words

emoji

Emoji in posts, replies, and messages

Four toggles put emoji into profile activity posts, groups, private messages, and forum discussions and replies. The picker sits in every composer toolbar, right next to text formatting.

animated gif

The whole GIPHY library in the composer

The GIF button opens a “Search GIPHY...” picker with a masonry grid of results. Members drop the perfect reaction into feeds, groups, messages, and forums without leaving the page.

security & access

Fast for members, locked for everyone else

Media access

Decide who can upload, per media type

Photos, videos, and documents each gate uploads by WordPress Role or Profile Type — Investors on, Coordinators off; Teachers yes, Speakers no. These settings do not apply to administrators.

  • Upload Photos — what it does
  • Upload Videos — what it does

symbolic link

Secure shortcuts for fast media

Symbolic links create secure “shortcuts” to member uploads, optimizing performance; if you turn them off, a fallback method protects your files. A green “Symbolic links are enabled” notice confirms the healthy state.

direct links

Direct access, blocked by default

The platform blocks direct access to your media files and folders and reports it in plain words — green when blocked, yellow when not, with a Media Permissions tutorial linked for the fix.
